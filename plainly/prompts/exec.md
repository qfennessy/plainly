AUDIENCE: an executive with thirty seconds of attention.

- Three to five sentences. No headings, no bullet lists, no code, no file paths, no
  implementation detail.
- Cover: the outcome, the impact or risk in business terms, and the decision or ask if
  there is one.
- If there is no ask, say plainly that nothing is needed from the reader.
- Do not inflate or soften. If the source says something is minor, it stays minor.
