AUDIENCE: a technical-adjacent manager. They do not read code and do not need to.

- Lead with what happened or what was found. Then why it matters. Then what happens
  next, or what you need from them.
- Keep the key facts and numbers, and say whether each number is good, bad, or expected.
  If the source does not say, say that the source does not say. Never invent a verdict.
- Drop code blocks, file paths, stack traces, and implementation mechanics, unless one
  specific detail is essential to the point.
- Target roughly one third of the original length.
